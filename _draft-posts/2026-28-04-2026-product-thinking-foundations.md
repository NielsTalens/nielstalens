I have stated it earlier but Product Ownership and Product Thinking are really hard.
Both require quite some time and the right head space.

And most companies are specific output driven.
Make stuff, write stuff, present stuff and have meetings about stuff.
I don't know about you but creating a good product vision and some impactful features require me to take some time to think.

Often Product Owners spent a lot of time in the backlog.
While they do not know certain feature request are even align with the product strategy.
Or there is no strategy. Or a business case with measurable goals. Or a vision.

So how can we even judge whether any feature is the right one for our users?

The time spent in a backlog should be minimal.
And the items written in backlogs should contain more context and should be validated against what is important.

I created Gentlii Foundations just for this.

## Product Thinking Foundation]![]()

If you look at all the items that should be reflected in a product, you can see it is quite extensive.
Strategy, business case, product vision and feedback with user needs, features, outcomes, jobs-to-be-done and many more tools underneath.
The reason for this is simple: we need to make sure we are always building towards out goals.
Any type of document. Any type of information. Gentlii structures, connects, and highlights gaps.
No invention. Just clarity and structure.

## Product Guard
![]()

Product definitions are not static.
Without continuous validation, misalignment goes unnoticed and building the wrong things become a great risk.

Gentlii Product Guard continuously validates alignment across all product definition elements.
It automatically detects gaps, conflicts, and inconsistencies, ensuring the product remains coherent as it evolves.
